FAQ

Good to know

Who registers the team?

The team leader. They fill in the form once and add the accounts of their teammates.

How many teammates can I add?

Up to six teammates, besides the leader.

When is the challenge?

The date is not announced yet. We will email the team leader as soon as it is confirmed, so use an email address you check often.

How do I know if my team is accepted?

Use "Track your registration" with the leader's email and team name. You will also get emails from us.

Can my team compete in several languages?

Each registration is for one language. To enter another language, register a new team.
